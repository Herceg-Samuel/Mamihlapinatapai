## Standards

Normal is a pretty low standard to live by.

Take a minute. Assess every thing you consider normal. Go on.

## List of normal stuff then

In the conversation, _George Mack_ references lists kept by the philosopher _Montaigne_ to illustrate how social norms are often just a form of "social contagion" that varies wildly between cultures.

Here are some of the practices mentioned that were considered normal in various societies throughout history (19:11-20:54):

- **Asymmetrical grooming:** It was once considered normal to grow hair out on the right side of the body while shaving everything on the left.
- **Teeth staining:** In certain cultures, white teeth were deemed unattractive, leading people to purposely stain their teeth blacker and blacker.
- **Ritualized patricide:** Within some societies, individuals were expected to kill their own fathers upon reaching a specific age.
- **Funerary consumption:** Some cultures practiced a funeral rite involving chopping up the deceased, grinding them into a pulp, mixing them into wine, and drinking the mixture on the day of the funeral.
- **Wedding customs:** Certain cultures judged the success of a wedding by how many of the groom’s friends were able to sleep with the bride.

## List of normal stuff now

In the conversation, _George Mack_ outlines a list of statistics and behaviors that he describes as what is considered 'normal' in modern society (21:11 - 22:01). These observations include:

- **Sugar Consumption:** Consuming 17 teaspoons of sugar per day.
- **Financial Debt:** Carrying $104,755 in debt.
- **Physical Health:** Being 30 lbs overweight.
- **Activity Levels:** Averaging less than 4,000 steps per day.
- **Work Engagement:** Spending 90,000 hours of life at work while disengaged.
- **Sedentary Lifestyle:** Staying indoors for 22.3 hours per day.
- **Toilet vs. Exercise:** Spending more time on the toilet than exercising.
- **Reading Habits:** Not finishing a single book in the last 12 months.
- **Silence:** Experiencing under 10 minutes of silence per day.
- **Screen Time:** Spending 20 years of life watching TV or scrolling social media.
