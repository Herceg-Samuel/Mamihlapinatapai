Entering the tunnel of enlightenment

Seeking the light

Searching for self preservation whilst gluttoning at the table self worth

Watching, gazing and frazzling with all but sore eyes, a broken spirit and a wallowing belly suffocated in crude expectation

Rushing through the motions, a tantalizing reality of causes

Wondering if the train is there to pick you up or it's just the promiscuous dead end in it's pre season.

A living, breathe sucking, unopposed contradiction to the perpetually advertised with loud shouts and crazy songs of pompous affirmations

Quickening jogs and runs in constant, unshakable, and unrelenting succession

Escaping the Manhattan

Scouring, scavenging the maniacal streets,

all crazy, muzzy, fuzzy and showering in the rain of hopeful disappointment awaiting the trenches of soaking unfulfillment
