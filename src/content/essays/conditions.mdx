# Unconditional love

This whole concept has always been so far. Foreign. Impossible. I mean you have to travel to infinity and back just to prove that you deserve it. Is it not the rare prize of the few worthy?

I was told Jesus loves unconditionally.
Pretty damn weird. Odd.
I mean why would he even do that?
Does he even know what that means? Like there is nothing that I could ever do to make him say, "Enough alright. I gave all and it just did not work. It was not enough. If only." Do you know how ridiculous that sounds? If you could see my face right now.

I had my short moment of love for someone.
I did not see it coming.
It did not make sense to me. Why should I be so happy that he found happiness?
That sadness did not make home in his heart! Depression, the destination no more.
He becomes a traveller eager to see the world(with or without me). TBH I prefer with. Cause it is much better.

What do you mean I felt like dying when his heart was broken?
When he lost hope and I could see him still fight. Force his eyes to remain open.
Maybe, just maybe, see what life is ahead.

All this is far from unconditional.
It just comes from the heart somewhere somewhere.
Yet there exists a love that is unconditional.
Love with no bounds.
I mean the stars are jealous and the sand of the sea envious. Infinity is awestruck. And the understanding of man damned!
