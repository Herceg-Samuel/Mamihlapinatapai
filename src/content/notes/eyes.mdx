following his eyes,
wonder as it may,
far and wide,
lost scarcely,
seeking constant,

following the beautiful,
ooh as it should,
there its rest lay,


it's refuge away,
from thine never,
oh what have I done,
of thine what has become,

who shall comfort this soul,
of hunger and thirst,
death lingers,
sorrow plunges,
