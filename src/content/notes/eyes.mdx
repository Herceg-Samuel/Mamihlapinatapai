following his eyes,

wonder as it may,

far and wide,

lost scarcely,

seeking constant,

<Pause />

following the beautiful,

ooh as it should,

there its rest lay,

<Pause />

it's refuge away,

from thine never,

oh what have I done,

of thine what has become,

<Pause />

who shall comfort this soul,

of hunger and thirst,

death lingers,

sorrow plunges,
