All that is in nature is natural, for what is not, is not in it!

There is no natural and unnatural nature, for whatever is found in nature is natural and whatever is natural appertains to nature.
Nature is what just is. What is. What is not is not and cannot be the case and what is is and cannot not be the case.

On this matter I was afraid, that perhaps I would find grounds for freedom, for the thing that troubles my heart would be liberated, and
as a man of chains, I would chase after another, only now stronger and more binding, for in this freedom, unforseen trouble awaits!

What is this matter of concern?
What by day is deemed unnatural and by night an abomination.
Both have been decimated by virtue of their context, the first as has already been shown, the latter, this same abomination befalls a man who keeps long hair and a woman who keeps it short.
Perhaps we have more people to kill!
