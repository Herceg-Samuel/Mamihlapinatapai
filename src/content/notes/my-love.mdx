Why my dearest, why my love,

I came to you with all but open arms,

But now scorn your daily serving,

Contempt a preemptive service,

What shall become of me,

All of land have I sought,

In you did I find refuge,

The lighthouse in this storm,

Shall you join those who oppose my people,

Ooh tyranny has become your sweet fruit,

How come your roots are now to be fetched by them fruits,

No sight no more,

The journey may as well cease,

What then ought I strive,

In this constrict has all been lost,

Indeed, it is all vanity!
