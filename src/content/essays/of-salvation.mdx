# Preface

The intentions indeed would be to banish the fear so inflicted on the many hearts that even dare inquire on such matters.
Fear is of the unknown. If it were the case that we are to know whether or not either; one or both can be lost(salvation
and the Holy Spirit), then we can be certain(certain used very loosely), and fear would no longer exist. It would be
action based on belief. Any belief has to be action inducing. And if we are to take the belief about A, then A would
directly or indirectly affect how we go about our lives, mostly the former holds.

Growing up the many sermons and teachings about how careful and watchful we all needed be for a time is coming that which
we know not when the Son of man is coming and woe to those who would not be ready. The foreacclaimed readiness was that of
action, one needed to be just righteous at the exact moment that they said goodbye to this foreign land of ours.

I am not certain why I found this deeply disturbing. Maybe because I could not see a world in which I could possibly be lucky
enough to be in right standing with God and at the same moment die. One thing that came to mind would be asking for forgiveness
of all my sins and then killing myself. This too, however, will lead me to the place of avoidance, for my undertakings possess
the very thing I avoid. Perhaps I could put myself in position where I do not kill myself but I end up dead. But this too, utterly
falls short. God knows the heart and intentions of man. So the conclusion would be that I am dammed!

I never thought of this. However, this would apply for every human being, unless by some luck they die in the right state(where
they ask forgiveness for all their sins and are fortunate do die in this state). Now, I am not sure I can repent of all sins,
that is, in the proper sense of the word “all“. I mean, this is what we are told, confess your sins and you will be forgiven.

Do we know all the sins that we have committed let alone repent of each and every one? We can only conclude that either we have
got this wrong or we are all damned, there is no salvation and Jesus practically could have enjoyed heaven and all its splendor
for in His coming, it has been deemed futile.

It would seem that God would not do anything in vain. We are told that the rain has to fulfill its purpose. The rain is a trivial
matter with respect to divine matters. We are God’s creation. He wants a relationship with us. We are to presuppose that He would
put measures that guarantee success for His intentions, at least for those who would come into agreement as He. It is impossible
that He would start all this preparation from the very beginning only for it to be fruitless. Jesus cursed an unfruitful fig tree
for its fruitlessness in a hostile situation, what more of the grand plan of the divine! Surely it holds and bears much fruit!

I wish not use many words. I only hope to use what God gave us, His word and reason, to investigate this matter(only know I trust
not either for this expedition!!). It may not be knowledge a priori but by reading and careful interpretation not based on what we
ought be but rather what was, is and will forever be. Jesus is truth and all truth is found in Him. Let that truth be made known
to us plainly, for in that can our beliefs be grounded and in & with rock solid foundation!

The Holy Spirit cannot be taken away from a Christian.
He exists in a person under one and only one condition; righteousness.
How is this righteousness accomplished?

sin and moral law ought not be conflated.

The old way involved sacrifice of bulls, goats and lambs for the forgiveness of sins.
When Jesus died, He fulfilled the righteous requirement of the law, that is, a sacrifice was offered.

What was required of a sacrifice for it to be holy and acceptable?

## The crossing

It's like the crossing of a river, there is a time one is on either side.
Righteousness becomes the being on the other side. That and only that.

It is not in the thought of crossing, planning the crossing or time of crossing. The goal is the landing on the other side, that is,
the only way one is considered having crossed to the other side.
It means that the forgiveness is not in the same manner as the thought, planning or process of it but the attainment.

The question of interest would be, if by some misfortune, the crossing man is met by an external force that impedes his crossing eg a
crocodile and his journey comes to a halt, would he by his good intentions be considered in the same manner as he who arrived?
For it was his deep desire to get to the other side, yet by no willing of himself his ambitions are cut short.

If we are to faithfully follow the argument, it would follow that the man has not attained the goal and he is no different to he who
never undertook the journey or he who never gave heed or he who heard of the other side and found no goodness and pleasure in both the
place and the destination, for both men reached not the land.

## Back to OT

We know however that there are no guarantees that the sins would be forgiven, the priests would be chained by the feet and if God did not
forgive the people, the priest would have to be dragged out(dead) as no sinner man could stay in the Holy of Holies.(there's a lot of
hand-waving concerning the circumstances but the matter of fact is, the priest could die if God did not accept the sacrifice,
if accepted, the priest continues with his life)

Did God ever reject forgiveness? Why would He? Why wouldn't He? Is there anyone that can say to Him that what He has said to Himself to do is wrong?
Would reason also fall short in this regard?

## Back to the river

There are certain properties of this river to be crossed:

1. No man by himself can cross it.

2. It is infinitely far away(by virtue of time and distance).

3. Only by the help of God can one cross and be on the other side.

4. The visit to the other side can be temporary(by animal sacrifice) or permanent(by Jesus)

5. It is full of challenges; deep waters, waves and storms, poaching animals and not to mention the infinity thereof.

About the two sides:

1. Every person begins his quest in the same side, let's call it A.

2. A is the default and is an undesirable state by God.

3. Every human ought strive to be away from A.

4. Choosing to remain in A is considered immoral and is punishable by all means including death.

The other side, we call it B.

1. B is the ultimate place to be, the goal, the end.

2. Every person is needed to desire B and by that desire they always are to be away from A, in the crossing.

3. B is both temporary and permanent in its residence.

4. The aim is permanent residency in B.

## The Heart of God

Before we move to the New Testament, let us examine the intentions of God. We deceive ourselves if we claim that we can with certainly know what God wills.
However, there are what God has spoken and shown time and time again.

God created man for the sake of being in relationship with Him. When sin entered into the world, man was separated from Him. It was God's desire that the relationship
be restored. He, being God, had a plan. The plan, Jesus. He sent His son for the forgiveness of sin and that we may have eternal life. Back to the famous verse:

"For God so loved the world that He gave His one and only son that whoever believes in Him shall not perish but have eternal life."

## Eternal life

Eternal life is the life in and of God. God is a timeless being. Others would say a temporal. He has no beginning nor end. The alpha and omega.

This is what He calls us to, life in and with Him. In a timeless, unending state.

This is a rather complicated matter, for how can he that is temporal enter and be temporal? That is only made possible by Jesus.

Jesus said, "I am the way, the truth and the life. No one comes to the Father except through me."

God had to provide a way where there seemed to be no way! And in this, we know to be true. We are going to posit and examine two different concepts;
being forgiven and saved, that is forgiveness and salvation.

## Forgiveness and salvation

Let us examine if they truly are one and the same.

Forgiveness, what is it? It is when the sinner man is declared righteous, when the sins are no longer attributed and imputed on him. How can one be forgiven?
By repentance, the change of heart.

There are several instances where man was forgiven.

1. Through OT sacrifice of bulls, sheep and goats, both for forgiveness of a person or a nation as done by priests(this has been shown).

2. Through baptism. John the Baptist, as is in the name, baptized people. He was the voice in the wilderness that prepared the way for the
   lamb of God. He preached rather fervently and told people to repent of their sins and be baptized. It has to be the case that the people after
   this event, as in the OT, would be forgiven. John was also in the priestly order. Zachariah, his father, was a priest. Priesthood was hereditary.
   To whether baptism or it being done by a priest(John by his being) was the reason for forgiveness, that I cannot ascertain.

3. The disciples forgiving of people's sins as sent by Jesus.

<Quote>
  21So Jesus said to them again, “Peace to you! As the Father has sent Me, I
  also send you.” 22And when He had said this, He breathed on them, and said to
  them, “Receive the Holy Spirit. 23If you forgive the sins of any, they are
  forgiven them; if you retain the sins of any, they are retained.”
  <Caption>John 20: 21-23</Caption>
</Quote>

4. The forgiveness that occurs between people. This is a different kind from what God does but it is commanded of us that inasmuch as we are forgiven of
   our sins, we ought also forgive others.

<Quote>
  14“For if you forgive men their trespasses, your heavenly Father will also
  forgive you. 15But if you do not forgive men their trespasses, neither will
  your Father forgive your trespasses.
  <Caption>Matthew 6:14-15</Caption>
</Quote>

5. Jesus's death on the cross. This however, is a different and unique case and that we shall deeply examine. It accomplishes the forgiveness aspect and
   on top as a superlative has salvation, or rather, is salvation in and of itself. It is the very essence of Jesus's work to be salvation and in the same
   way permanent as will be briefly shown.

Salvation, what is it? It is when the sinner man is given a new nature, becomes a new creation, one with no sin and its capacity.

Salvation with it comes forgiveness. The opposite however, does not hold. Why? Forgiveness was a temporary thing for a particular thing or set of things.

Suppose with me, that you or I or another, as in the OT, were to go to the temple and offered sacrifices for forgiveness of our sins. What sins would be forgiven?
It would those that have already taken place, that is, past sins. And after this sacrifice, the man 1 or 5 seconds or any further amount of time later, commits a sin.
Would that now committed sin be forgiven? Surely not. The sacrifice covered what was already done and never what would be done later, whether seconds, minutes, hours or
whatever unit of time that would exist in the future of that moment X.

This then leads us to the temporal nature of the forgiveness. It is only payment for the debt that had been accrued and any further debt that comes into play needs a payment
of its own. It would be improper(absurd even) to assume that if after taking a loan from the bank and thereafter repay it that you have been established to as having  
repaid all future accruable debts!

Is salvation deemed truly different from forgiveness? How? It be so by its permanence. What does this mean? Its activity always holds for all time. It is like an
indestructible item, there is by definition, no destruction nor temporality in it.

## The game

I wish to posit that reality is synonymous to a game of chance, where you become "lucky" in being in a position of gain. I in by no means suggest that he who is never in
the game has the same odds as he who plays. He, however, might have a better position with regards to potential lose. I tread carefully with this matter as they do not get
mapped one to one in a clean manner yet a truth is to be found in this.

The man who never comes to belief is like he who never comes into the casino. He is not in a position to win and is dammed to a lose of sorts given the qualification for
success in this world of ours is a win in the casino.
He who believes enters this casino and begins to play. He starts with a significant amount and the odds of success are incredibly high. It like a 99.999...9% chance of success.
From probability and statistics, we need to appreciate the matter of fact, success is not guaranteed. It is very high but never inevitable. A loss is possible and when it
occurs, one is rendered hopeless. He has no more chips to play and by no means can he get another chance as he has already utilized what he was given freely and with the
highest of odds yet he succumbed. Surely no more can be done to and for such a person. He is by all means finally kicked out of the casino, no success; the win and definitely
no chance of round two. He remains out with those who never played and are both doomed to life in deep anguish and suffering. A life with no hope. A life not worth living.

I would agree that most men would want to try their luck. The master of the casino, however, knows the hearts and intents of every player who comes knocking.
Does he let every man in? Does he gives words of advice to them that enter? Word has it that he gives one like himself as helper to every man, to help them in this game
to ensure a guaranteed success. The helper is full of all wisdom there is to be obtained with regards to the play. Can the helper with all his wisdom be at a position
of a loss? He guides, instructs and teaches yet never makes the man do what is not in his willing. He takes a yes and no in the same manner, with respect for the mans willing!
He is what we call a gentleman. One of the highest kind.

Another case would be made that the odds are in actuality reversed. The chances of surviving this game of chance grow infinitely small and the fore proclaimed message
of "assurance" is made inept.

# Of actions

Beliefs are action inducing. What a man believes, that, dictates his actions.

In the same way, given our freedom in Christ from the shackles of sin and death, we also live in the same manner.

Fruits come from trees. Fruits are produced of the same kind as the tree itself. In the same way an apple tree would never produce oranges, so then life can
never produce death and light nay darkness. For as from the source, all flows. So also what is in it remains in the same manner necessarily(unless polluted).

Actions of man always come short, and by them no salvation can be conceived. One cannot loose that which He did not get by their actions, by actions.

Actions are induced and produced, never necessary conditions. The needed conditions have been fulfilled by & in Christ. Now we only bear the fruits of His labor.
