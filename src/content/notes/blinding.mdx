Blinding they say are for fools,

an exception of the same is afforded to those men of many professings,

Only you are not expected not gauge your own eyes,

faith is a substance, it is evidence,

do not be fooled,

keep your eyes, keep your senses,

they remain useful,

if they make you stumble, offer to your maker,

never to the man of demands,

he will ask, take and leave, the devil their model,

fathered indeed!

<Pause />
Perhaps you don't need your eyes, faith lingers!
