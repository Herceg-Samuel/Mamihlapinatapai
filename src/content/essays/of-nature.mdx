If a society prides itself in action, agency
and responsibility, why would it depend on nature
as a stamp for what ought happen? That is, the right
and its violation, the wrong or evil. The moral and immoral.

Why is it more noble to die of old age than in combat?
Why is nature consulted on matters ethics and morality?

Isn't nature just what is? An observation
and never a prescription. It shows what is,
what exists, and never what ought exist.

If we had observed the complete opposite of
what we now do of nature, would we in the
same manner speak high its praises and
adjust our lives on it?

Even now, it is more natural to kill people.
Animals do that. Even some plant.
The third person on earth killed the fourth.

Nature is not a pill of recommendation to swallow.
It is not a careful instructor in the ways we should go.
It carries no oughts and flourishing is not in its
nature(I dare use the word).

Children naturally steal, lie and are selfish.
They are taught to do what is against what is
engrained in themselves. They are to share, always
tell the truth and always respect people's property.

Normal is but another disease, it flows from a collection
of people following a corrupt nature. Popular opinion is
nothing but popular. It is the sum of the shouts of many.

It is natural to let you hair just grow, never cut your finger
and toenails, baths are for the unnatural.
