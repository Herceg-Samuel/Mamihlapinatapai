Logical bugs are the absolute worst.

Someone said,Elliot, debugging is more about finding the error. Less fixation on fixing. You can't fix what you do not know.

Logical bugs.

They are correct enough not to be caught by any compiler. It is truthy. Just not there. Borderline honesty.

Trying to debug such is an absolute nightmare. You'd swear you are dyslesxic. The moment you go through your code to just to find you left out one letter. You had it in a different place. Maybe you added another.

We create, add, update and remove. You'd think we are carrying out CRUD operations on our thoughts. Though accurate in a number of ways.

I wish there was a way to detect all this. Nowadays, just throw the entire thing to an AI. It is built to do better stuff. However, they will diligently tell you how you left out an A or added an X. Then you carry all the corrected code, hoping it has not been further altered, and paste it. Vuallah. The program runs. Well, you hope. Otherwise you have such other errors in other places.

In life, we walk with logical errors. Errors in thinking. I am right. You are definitely wrong. I know. I am sure. I am sure, right.

It is even worse when the logical error goes unnoticed. The code works just ok. You did the tests and somehow it just worked out. Unnoticed.

It comes knocking one day. They call it technical debt, well, most of them we get to see and put as a to fix later, then we forget.

It haunts you when you find 20 missed calls from the client. You know all hell is now loose. You look again. Stare at the phone. Do I find the issue? Can I? What did I do? Something is wrong?

Just when you are about to decide, the phones vibrates arrogantly, hinting that you are in deep sh\*t. It moves slowly towards you. Take hold of me. It shouts. You are in trouble. It makes sure you know.

You take the deep breaths you were told would reduce your stress levels. Then there goes your arm. Outstretched. Competing with your phone at its game. Shaking.

You took too long. It goes off. 21 missed calls. The message comes. Pick the phone. What did you do?

You are in awe. No. Perplexed. What do you mean? Well, you can't really ask that.

There is no such checker. A reliable one. The closest ones, we still debate about. Debate is such a nice word. We argue. Hate. Divide. Is it relevant? Modern? Does it cover today's issues?

We live as know it alls. Sometimes we doubt ourselves enough. Then the motivational speaker says you are right. You know all the facts about you. Nothing is wrong with you. Here's the kicker, you are perfect, just as you are.

Your logical errors. They blind you. We can't call you foolish or a bastard. You have your rights. We think about it enough.

We remember we share in the same. Arrogant, privileged, narcissistic.
