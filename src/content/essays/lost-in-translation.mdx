A lot gets lost in translation, in conversations, stories, casting of books to tv shows & films, even what we have in our minds to words spoken among many other examples.

Most is lost because we do not have the necessary complete means for the achievement of our goals. We do not have the means and the language for this sort of activities.

The communication utterly falls short.

We do not know enough words to tell ourselves let alone others what we feel.

Emotions are complex.
Language is varied. It differs across cultures . It continues to grow both apart.

It is communicated in different ways as well, in words, actions, facial expressions, many others and their combinations.

In English, for example, we get very specific words for specific emotions. We however do not know most of them.

We rely on the maybe 10 to 50 that are familiar to us.

Try naming all emotions you use!

### The Chosen

Listened to Alex O'Connor interviewing The Chosen creator, Dallas Jenkins(2hrs 15 minutes).

It was very clear from the onset that even in the telling a story like that of Jesus, not everything in the very detail can be showcased. Mostly because we also do not have all the details about the life during the time of Jesus.

Matters on how Jesus interacted with people, including the disciples is in most times unrecorded. The actual events took place for around 3 years. John recorded that if all Jesus did for those 3 years, the entire world would not be able to contain it.

<Quote>
  "Jesus did many other things as well. If every one of them were written down,
  I suppose that even the whole world would not have room for the books that
  would be written."

</Quote>

There are also conflicting ideas found in the narrations from the 4 gospels of Matthew, Mark, Luke and John.
